About Guardian SOS

Your personal safety, emergency communication, location awareness and journey tracking companion.

Personal Safety

Guardian SOS brings together safety tools that help users access emergency features, manage trusted contacts, review safety-related history and stay informed about their journeys.

Emergency SOS

The SOS feature allows a user to initiate an emergency request through Guardian SOS. When location access is available, the request can include the user's GPS coordinates to help emergency contacts understand their location.

Important: SOS does not guarantee that an alert will be delivered, received or answered. Delivery may depend on internet or mobile network availability, location permissions and the operation of the service.

Emergency Contacts

Add and manage the people you trust for emergency situations. Contact information can include relationship details and priority settings, helping you organize your emergency contacts.

Keep contact details accurate and make sure your trusted contacts understand their role.

My Location

Guardian SOS can use your device's location permission to obtain your current location when supported and available. Location information can support safety features and help you understand where you are.

GPS accuracy and availability can vary depending on your device, permissions, surroundings and connectivity.

Live Journey Tracking

Users can view the map before starting a journey and enter journey details, including a destination and travel information. When a journey is started, the available tracking features can help display the route, current location, remaining distance and estimated arrival time.

Journey information depends on available location and routing data. The user travelling should use the journey controls provided in the app to manage their journey.

Chat and Communication

Guardian SOS includes chat features for communication between users. Depending on availability, supported features include real-time messages, attachments, voice messages, and voice or video calling.

Successful delivery and call quality can depend on network connectivity, device permissions and service availability.

Weather Forecasts

The weather feature lets users search for locations and view available weather information and forecasts. This can help with planning activities and journeys.

Weather information depends on the availability and freshness of the weather data service.

Safety Center

The Safety Center brings together available safety-related information and controls. Depending on the features enabled in your version, these can include safety status, battery information, family-circle tools, privacy controls and location-related tools.

SOS and Location History

History pages help users review available records of previous SOS events and location information. The records displayed depend on what was saved successfully and what is available in the user's account.

Account and Profile

Your profile provides access to available account information and profile settings. Keep your information up to date and protect access to your account.

Maps and Navigation

Guardian SOS uses Leaflet to display interactive maps with OpenStreetMap and Esri map layers. These map technologies support the map display used by the app.

Note: The map display is based on Leaflet and the configured OpenStreetMap and Esri layers; it should not be described as a Google Maps integration.

Important Safety Information

Guardian SOS is a safety-support tool. It is not a substitute for local emergency services or professional emergency assistance. Do not rely on the app as your only way to request help.

Keep your phone charged, enable the necessary permissions, maintain accurate emergency contact details and contact local emergency services directly when necessary.
