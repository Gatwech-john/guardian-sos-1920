About Guardian SOS

Emergency assistance and personal safety.

🛡️ Personal Safety


Guardian SOS is designed to provide quick access to emergency assistance features when you need them.

🚨 Emergency SOS


The SOS feature creates an emergency request through the Guardian SOS system and can include your current GPS coordinates.

👥 Trusted Contacts


You can store emergency contacts and manage their relationship and priority.

📍 Location


Guardian SOS can use your browser's GPS location permission to save your coordinates and display them on Google Maps.

🚨 Activate SOS?

This will create an emergency SOS request. Your current location will be included if GPS access is available.
